We apply this workflow to Cu-In-Se, the system of the solar absorber CuInSe2, and search for low-energy structures on both the Cu-rich and the Cu-poor side. findings: - claim: "Humans define the tasks, and the agent carries them out." detail: >- From a single request, the agent completed the first task in 183 steps with no further human input. The authors reviewed the scientific validity of the outputs and extended the checks where the automated runs were incomplete. - claim: "The agent joins different methods in one workflow." detail: >- Structure generation with MatterGen and icet, machine-learning potentials, DFT with VASP, phonon calculations with Phonopy and a band-gap model. The agent writes the Python that connects them and runs the jobs on a supercomputer. - claim: "The search reveals an electronic asymmetry in Cu-In-Se." detail: >- Within 25 meV per atom of the reference hull, the Cu-rich side holds 202 structures, all classified as metallic, and 198 of them have no match in the Materials Project, OQMD, Alexandria or GNoME.
